Supplementary shift.

Today was quite busy at the clinic; way too many patients coming in sick. One came in post sailing the seas, looking for a cure to their gum disease. Another, in the RAF they flew, seeking a way to make their vision true. The third craved chokeberries, hoping this scent would attract bees. Next had so much dandruff on his pillow, so I prescribed him to chew upon some willow. Following had ulcers whose stomach they ravaged, for them I mixed barley and cabbage. Then a lady brought rats saying they wouldn’t lactate, but this really should’ve been the vet’s case. The last walked in with an ataxic gate, so I prescribed some drugs so they wouldn’t oxidate.
